Symptoms are clues—not diagnoses

Flickering lights, dead receptacles, repeated trips, buzzing equipment, or partial power loss can have different causes. A systematic evaluation may include the affected circuit, connections, devices, breakers, panel condition, load behavior, and related equipment. Replacing a visible component without locating the cause can leave the underlying problem unresolved.

Helpful information before service

  • When the problem started and whether it is constant or intermittent.
  • Which rooms, circuits, appliances, or machines are affected.
  • What was operating immediately before the symptom appeared.
  • Whether there is heat, odor, sound, discoloration, or water exposure.
  • Any recent construction, equipment changes, or previous repair attempts.

Do not remove a panel cover or touch damaged electrical equipment to collect details.

Troubleshooting questions

Why does a breaker keep tripping?

Possible causes include excessive demand, a short circuit, a ground fault, damaged wiring, faulty equipment, or a breaker issue. Testing is needed to identify the cause.

Should I reset a breaker repeatedly?

No. Repeated tripping is a protective response. Stop using the affected circuit and arrange an evaluation, especially if there is heat, odor, buzzing, or visible damage.
