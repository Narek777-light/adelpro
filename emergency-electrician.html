Know when to call public emergency services first

Adelpro is an electrical contractor, not a replacement for 911, the fire department, or the utility emergency line. For fire, smoke, injury, downed power lines, or immediate danger, leave the area if appropriate and call 911 or the utility first. Do not enter water near energized equipment and do not touch a fallen line.

Information that helps dispatch

  • The service address and county.
  • A safe callback number.
  • What you observed and when it started.
  • Whether the issue affects part or all of the property.
  • Any smoke, odor, buzzing, sparks, heat, water, or visible damage.

Dispatch availability does not promise a particular arrival time. Conditions, location, access, and crew availability can affect response.

Call about urgent symptoms

  • Burning odor or unusual heat
  • Buzzing or arcing sounds
  • Repeated trips with visible damage
  • Partial or total power loss
  • Water near electrical equipment

Emergency dispatch questions

Is Adelpro open 24/7 for regular appointments?

No. Regular business hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Emergency dispatch is the service available 24/7.

Should I use the web form during immediate danger?

No. Leave the area when appropriate and call 911 or the relevant utility emergency line. Do not rely on an online form for immediate safety assistance.

Where is dispatch available?

Adelpro's stated service area is Los Angeles County and Orange County. Call to confirm availability for the specific address.
